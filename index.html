Debamita Ghosh

I develop learning algorithms that make reliable decisions from limited information—and continue to do so as environments change, uncertainties arise, and challenges evolve.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Central Florida, working with Prof. George K. Atia and Prof. Yue Wang. My research is motivated by the sim-to-real gap, where discrepancies between training environments and real-world conditions challenge the reliability of autonomous decision-making systems. To address this gap, I develop mathematical foundations and efficient algorithms for online distributionally robust sequential decision-making, spanning single-agent and multi-agent reinforcement learning. My work brings together reinforcement learning, online learning, optimization, and game theory to design theoretically grounded, sample-efficient, and scalable algorithms with provable guarantees under model misspecification, limited data, and strategic uncertainty.

Prior to joining UCF, I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Bombay through the IITB–Monash Research Academy, under the supervision of Prof. Manjesh Kr. Hanawal and Prof. Nikola Zlatanov. My doctoral research focused on sample-efficient online learning for beam alignment and beamforming under uncertain wireless channels and resource constraints, earning the Naik and Rastogi Best PhD Thesis Award and the Coromandel International Best PhD Thesis Award. Building on these foundations, my current research extends beyond wireless systems to address fundamental challenges in learning, exploration, and coordination under model misspecification and strategic uncertainty. This progression reflects a unifying theme throughout my work: enabling reliable decision-making from limited and imperfect information.

Research vision

Advancing the foundations of Trustworthy Autonomous Intelligence

My long-term goal is a unified theoretical and algorithmic framework in which autonomous agents learn, adapt, and collaborate, with rigorous guarantees on

  • Robustness
  • Reliability
  • Scalability
  • Generalization

To build it, I bring together ideas from

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Online learning
  • Statistical learning theory
  • Optimization
  • Game theory
